# ADR-0028: Reviewed preproduction group coach compensation precedence

**Scope:** Separate source approval under [ADR-0020](0020-review-exact-preproduction-schema-successors.md). This change replaces only the permit and records the exact decision. It leaves the installed baseline, transition manifest, lock, guard and workflow unchanged. It does not authorize release, deployment, payment operations, live migration or operational cutover.

## Decision

For a fresh non-fixed coach occurrence approval, applicable active group coach terms take precedence over a workplace-wide fallback. Use the completed session's frozen group and workplace, the authenticated teacher and actual taught local day. Reject a broad selection that needs group review rather than selecting different terms or a payer automatically; multiple applicable active group terms fail closed. Exact lesson coach precedence remains higher. This change does not broaden approval on student-specific, private recurrence, participant, sponsor, fixed monthly, invoice, credit, cash or provider paths. Existing finalized rows and exact request replay remain unchanged, including a prior valid broad approval when a competing group contract is accepted later.

Group term edits, including archival, serialize with applicable fresh posting using the existing venue compensation lock. Fresh coach approval retains the existing READ COMMITTED requirement; exact historical replay remains first. The inherited direct repeatable-read terms path can still produce overlapping rows from old snapshots. The fresh approval fence refuses both broad and group selections if this ambiguity exists; it does not claim absolute storage-level uniqueness.
