## Exact authority and lifecycle

This source increment supports one explicitly confirmed sponsor account for one teacher-owned canonical completed occurrence and one frozen participant. The dedicated authenticated owner RPC creates or archives the exact participant agreement under ownership, revision and replay checks; raw table writes cannot mint this override. Existing dated payer matching remains required for ordinary participant approvals. An active exact sponsor requires the reviewed exact agreement rather than silently using the default. Historic coach compensation and other occurrences and participants remain separate. Approval of terms does not create an invoice, credit, receipt, payment, Portal grant or provider state; posted obligations retain frozen terms and payer.

The protected participant review witness is monotonic. Archived sponsorship continues to quarantine legacy automatic invoice and redemption for that exact participant occurrence. If the sponsor was archived before posting, a newly reviewed native dated-default obligation can still be approved; this is not permission for legacy automatic fallback. Portable JSON carrying exact sponsor or protected witness is deliberately rejected atomically with an operator-pack requirement; it is not approval provenance. Existing guarded invoice or redemption receipts replay before new row checks. The direct disposable database erasure probe found ordinary no-finance deletion unchanged and the existing protected-finance dependency still rejects exact scoped rows; no app erasure workflow or financial-history erasure is established.
