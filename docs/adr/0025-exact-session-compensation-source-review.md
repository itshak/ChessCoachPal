# ADR-0025: Reviewed preproduction exact-session coach compensation successor

**Scope:** Approve only the exact source-lock successor listed below under [ADR-0020](0020-review-exact-preproduction-schema-successors.md). This approval-only change records the decision and replaces the permit; the installed baseline, manifest, active lock, and protected verification remain unchanged. This does not authorize release, deployment, operational cutover, or any real financial action.

## Design and scope

Add an optional typed exact-session agreement for a single owned, completed canonical occurrence and its owned workplace. This increment is for coach earnings only. It preserves broader existing scopes when no active exact agreement applies, but a new coach approval cannot bypass an active exact contract by selecting a broader one. A selected stale, mismatched, archived, or otherwise ineligible exact agreement requires review; no silent fallback. Currency is set against the venue default at creation and remains pinned independently of later default changes; the same-currency rate may still be explicitly revised before posting. Posting requires the active owned payer under the reviewed lock order. The new scoped terms, exact lesson identity and revision are preserved in the immutable obligation; archival preserves exact replay.

No new client execution grant is added. The guarded trigger function is owner-only; existing owner RLS and grants continue. Participant sponsorship, recurring schedule agreements, legacy migration, and web/MCP management UX for exact agreements are outside this increment. No change to what is considered received money.
