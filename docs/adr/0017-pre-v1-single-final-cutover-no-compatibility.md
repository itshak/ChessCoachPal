# ADR-0017: One final pre-v1 cutover, no compatibility layer

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Status:** Accepted — binding clarification from the product owner
- **Supersedes:** ADR-0015's per-feature **production** transition cadence and any interpretation of ADR-0016 that requires retaining obsolete Family/Studio/guardian/portal contracts. ADR-0001's no-backward-compatibility principle is reaffirmed.
- **Does not supersede:** Tenant authorization, data privacy, off-host backup/recovery, teacher-owned history semantics, MCP/website parity, or the requirement for explicit permission before touching a live database.

## Context

ChessCoachPal is pre-v1, at an early development/beta stage. Building compatibility shims for CogNote or earlier ChessCoachPal tables, routes, account models, exports, tool names, Family Portal tokens or Studio settings would add permanent complexity without serving a supported API contract. The owner is willing to export/reimport existing data or rebuild the present early database instead of paying the compatibility cost of a full historical conversion.

## Decision

1. **Single final target.** Implement planned, approved OpenSpec changes against **one** clean canonical fresh-install baseline (`supabase/migrations/20261107000000_chesscoachpal_baseline.sql`) and matching generated types, seeds, API/MCP, RLS, exports and tests. Do not accumulate old and new production contracts or introduce version negotiation, aliases, compatibility tables/views, dual writes or legacy token endpoints solely to accommodate pre-v1 releases. Update the website and MCP together.
2. **Defer real database alteration to one coordinated cutover.** Feature development and isolated synthetic database tests can proceed in slices, but **do not apply each slice's intermediate migrations to the real deployment**. Once the integrated target and verification gates are ready, choose precisely one operational path:
   - **Path A — one reviewed data transformation:** a bounded, one-time migration from the *observed* deployed source schema directly to the final canonical target; preserve selected student/lesson/financial records as explicitly mapped, check counts and scopes, then retire all obsolete structures.
   - **Path B — clean rebuild/reimport (explicitly acceptable):** take and verify an off-host backup/export, provision an empty database from the **final baseline**, and import the teacher-owned data the operator elects to retain. This may intentionally leave behind unneeded development records; omissions and reimport semantics must be visible and approved before switching production traffic.
3. **No historical permission inheritance.** Old guardian or Family membership and possession of an old household token SHALL NOT become a new scoped portal grant. On cutover, retire prior tokens and issue new grants only following explicit teacher authorization of the exact person, student, workplace, resource permissions and expiration. A contact or payer relationship alone never grants view/cancel/report or payment access.
4. **Do not preserve old entry points.** The final app shall use Contacts, independent Billing Accounts, Workplaces, and a scoped optional Student & Contact Portal. Remove old `guardians`, `family`/`studio` primary domain routes, old token handlers, compatibility RPCs and schema once the replacement business workflows pass parity/security tests. Necessary domain functions (e.g. lessons, payments, events) must continue in the *new model*, not through adapters into the old model.
5. **Development artifacts are not a live migration plan.** Any earlier staged SQL or historical CI convergence fixture is an implementation/test artifact, not permission or a requirement to replay an incremental migration chain in production. Before final cutover, collapse the candidate new-install schema and produce the exact approved final one-time operator procedure; remove obsolete operational migrations and checks rather than shipping generic pre-v1 migration compatibility.
6. **Live safety remains mandatory.** This ADR does **not** itself authorize a production reset, data deletion, migration, live portal revocation, or deployment. A separately verified target, permission, off-host backup and tested restore/reimport are required before changing a real database. Keep private source data, backups, tokens and operational secrets out of the public repository and CI fixtures.
7. **Acceptance.** Fresh-baseline bootstrap and website/MCP parity; isolated tenant and portal authorization; deterministic imports/exports and ID mapping; reports/financial history if retained; tested recovery; and complete CI/E2E/security/Compose smoke. Record what was retained or intentionally discarded. Archive individual OpenSpec changes only after their actual implemented contracts are accepted; historical branches/PRs are not acceptance evidence by themselves.

## Rationale and consequences

The goal is a clean, coherent pre-v1 product rather than a universal updater for unfinished schemas. A clean rebuild may be simpler and safer than writing and validating a large historical translation. The tradeoff is a planned cutover window, fresh credentials/grants and possible intentional reimport effort. This is acceptable before v1, but no data may be silently erased and no old portal viewer may silently receive expanded rights. ADR-0015 remains a record of earlier completed source-level work, not the controlling cadence for this future final rollout.
