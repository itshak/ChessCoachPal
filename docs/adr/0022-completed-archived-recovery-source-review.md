# ADR-0022: Reviewed preproduction completed archived-learner recovery successor

**Scope:** Approves only the exact preproduction source-lock successor below, through [ADR-0020](0020-review-exact-preproduction-schema-successors.md). The reviewed main baseline and its active guard do not change in this approval-only commit. This decision does not authorize a deployment, live schema migration or restore, release, beta cutover, billing activation, or changes to archived credit/payment-settlement policy.

## Product decision

A coach may review and attest a genuinely elapsed same-day new lesson in an active owned workplace and explicitly reactivate approved archived learners in the same transaction. The supported shapes are archived-learner one-to-one and mixed archived/active roster. The reviewed local time must map uniquely to the recorded venue timezone and UTC offset. Start and end must be elapsed on the reviewed current day in both the teacher enrollment zone and the venue zone. This is not historical reconstruction, a retroactive enrollment, or a generic historical edit.

The transaction resolves the exact owner-scoped reviewed learners, freshness/effective enrollment, prior verified archived membership and quota, creates only new dated enrollment periods as applicable, creates one new frozen lesson roster and records only explicit participant outcomes (present, late or excused, with at least one actual attendee). It creates no automatic credits, charges, payment allocations, notes, mastery or external effects. It does not change independently reviewed billing or credit settlement. Recovery checks both scheduled and completed intervals. The paired active-only completed-group writer shares the advisory lock and rejects overlapping archived-recovery provenance; this does not claim a global calendar uniqueness rule across separate legacy workflows. Exact approved replay returns the original receipt; changed terms or stale review are rejected.
