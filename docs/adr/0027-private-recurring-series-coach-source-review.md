# ADR-0027: Reviewed preproduction private recurring-series coach compensation successor

**Scope:** Approval-only under [ADR-0020](0020-review-exact-preproduction-schema-successors.md). This decision replaces the exact permit; the installed baseline, transition manifest, source lock and protected checks remain unchanged until a separate activation. It does not authorize release, deployment, live migration, payment operations or operational cutover.

## Exact scope and precedence

This source increment gives a teacher-owned **private recurring series** an explicit coach-earnings agreement. Canonical recurring materialization and the reviewed unmaterialized one-date move retain the existing series root and original local occurrence day; the stamped occurrence identity remains stable across split segments and slot detachment. Agreement applicability uses the actual taught local date, not the original recurrence date. A reviewed cross-venue move does not carry the old venue's series authority. Admission checks intersection with an active owned segment's date interval, not whether that interval contains an actual matching weekday occurrence. No historical backfill or inferred series identity: where an unproven older recurrence could be affected, broad approval fails closed rather than silently choosing a rate. A verified exact-lesson coach agreement takes precedence. The new scope does not introduce group recurrence or change participant/sponsor authority, automatic invoices, make-up credits, payments, provider state or teaching eligibility.

Only the reviewed owner routines can create or archive prospective series terms and approve a scope-specific earning. They check the owned series and venue, terms/currency, reviewed revision or amount, and exact replay. Activated terms and posted snapshots are preserved. Generic native approval does not adopt or replay scoped obligations; the previously merged #153 browser/MCP compatibility bridge withholds scoped terms and potential overlapping broad previews from old surfaces. Portable JSON carrying the new protected provenance or terms is not accepted as a replacement for the operator pack. This approval neither adopts the new workflow in the app nor authorizes a history rewrite.
