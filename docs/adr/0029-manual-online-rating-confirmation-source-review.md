# ADR-0029: Reviewed preproduction manual online rating confirmation binding

**Scope:** Approval only under [ADR-0020](0020-review-exact-preproduction-schema-successors.md). This change records the exact decision and replaces the permit. The installed baseline, transition manifest, active lock, guards and workflows remain unchanged. No application adoption, automatic ratings, durable opt-in, live migration, release or deployment is approved here.

## Decision

A fresh teacher-confirmed public Lichess or Chess.com refresh must bind the exact owned active student, saved public username, reviewed semantic categories and values, and a database-generated student binding epoch. A private noncycling sequence advances on issuance and on actual changes to online handles or archive state; unrelated profile edits do not advance the epoch. Deleting and recreating an otherwise unreferenced student ID does not reuse its epoch. The two service-only routines read the epoch as a decimal string and admit the bounded batch atomically after rechecking the binding. Provider/ingestion timestamps remain separate observation metadata, not part of the semantic review digest. The linked handle is not proof of account ownership or skill.

The shared teacher/request claim prevents a committed key from being reopened across evidence sources or disjoint categories. Claims are sealed at commit, stay private, and survive privileged snapshot cleanup; removing only an authentication identity does not erase the separate domain teacher. Existing domain teacher deletion cascades claims after inherited history dependencies are cleared. Historic snapshots remain evidence; existing exact receipts replay before changing-student/fetch decisions in the separate application patch, and exact committed new SQL receipts can replay under REPEATABLE READ. Fresh one-shot SQL admission requires READ COMMITTED; uncontended existing lower direct writes were proven at REPEATABLE READ, and a stale competing snapshot cannot extend the sealed claim.

The worker remains separate and still requires its existing service role, durable consent, queued job and lease. Rating writes take teacher authority before student, with a bounded NOWAIT student lock and typed contention result, then the tenant/request lock; this is not a claim about all external lock orders or absolute deadlock freedom. Existing manual-entry, official federation, worker and lower role authority are not expanded.
