## Open scope and operational gates

- [ ] Resolve the amendment's completed-lesson wording before calling the feature complete. The verified atomic recovery creates **one future scheduled** lesson with unmarked attendance. The frozen writer has no attendance payload, refuses past start timestamps and cannot combine reactivation with a completed/full historical lesson. Existing completed capture rejects archived learners; Notes and attested historical dates are independent pathways. The schema-preserving option is an explicit product decision to support future scheduled recovery with separate later attestation. If combined completed recovery is required, review a separate schema decision; chaining existing writes would not be atomic. Acceptance would verify one fully attested completed roster, and that a failed quota, schedule or attendance check leaves no lesson, membership, reactivation or attendance changes.
- [ ] Reconcile the original blanket archived credit-hold language with verified work performed before archive. New lessons and purchases cannot be granted from archive; archive itself leaves credit/expiry/funding untouched. The allocator evaluates the earned lesson date, not the later invoice date, and can insert a first redemption for already-attested eligible pre-archive work. That is different from exact receipt replay or recognizing an already redeemed participant, and the frozen redemption guard does not check current archive state. Recommended distinction for review only: settle independently verified usage earned before archive without reactivation, while blocking new use during archive. No such policy choice is approved here. Review whether historically earned first settlement is explicitly preserved or all new redemptions are held until reactivation. The latter needs an authoritative database rule; an app-only filter would not enforce it. Test both already-applied replay and eligible historical work not yet redeemed, plus denial of new lesson/purchase and unchanged cross-ledger state. No new cash or eligibility authority is approved.
- [ ] For the one real transition, reconcile actual populated-beta enrollment dates, historical attendance, payment/payer obligations and legacy record provenance on the specifically approved target. Historical facts alone must never manufacture current enrollment. Independently restore the actual off-host backup and obtain operator authorization under [ADR-0017](../../../docs/adr/0017-pre-v1-single-final-cutover-no-compatibility.md) and the [runbook](../../../docs/SCHEMA_TRANSITION_RUNBOOK.md). No live data, release, cutover, provider activation, deployment or customer self-host acceptance is implied by synthetic checks.

Keep this change active. The verified merged source checkpoint does not flatten unsupported product behavior or the operational checkboxes. The next independently source-ready proposal need not wait for a live cutover.
