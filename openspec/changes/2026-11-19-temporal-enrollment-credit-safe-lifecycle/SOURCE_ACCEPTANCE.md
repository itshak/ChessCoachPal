## Accepted same-day completed recovery

Future scheduled recovery remains the default and does not mark attendance. The separate **Completed today** flow supports one archived learner or an explicitly mixed active/archived roster in an owned active venue. The coach reviews exact identities, independently verified prior enrollment for each returning learner, current effective enrollment for active learners, quota and schedule. The database rechecks the exact dated review, venue zone and UTC offset, unique local mapping, and that both start and end already elapsed on the same current day in the teacher enrollment and venue time zones. Each returning learner must have been archived before the lesson started; enrollment is effective from the reviewed teacher-local date, never backdated.

The coach attests Present, Late or Excused individually (at least one Present or Late), then separately gives a reason and approves. The commit is atomic across reactivation, new dated enrollment, one new frozen roster and explicit attendance. It rejects overlap, changed review, stale revision, unsupported attendance and quota without partial writes. All-archived groups, pre-archive or earlier-day reconstruction, ambiguous DST times, cross-midnight lessons, unattended rosters, absent and cancellation outcomes are not supported by this recovery. Existing canonical Notes and historical attendance evidence remain separate and do not reactivate anyone. The recovery does not automatically redeem or grant credits, charge, invoice, add notes/material evidence or notify. Ordinary separately reviewed finance is unchanged.

The signed browser and delegated MCP proof covers read-only review, actual keyboard approval, a committed write whose response was lost, disabled edits while confirmation was pending, byte-identical retry with the same request ID, focused receipt, changed-body conflict under the same request ID, and authenticated state readback. [ADR-0022](../../../docs/adr/0022-completed-archived-recovery-source-review.md) records matching disposable PG 17.12 Format 3 snapshots and an independent checksum-sealed physical restore. A direct authenticated SQL replay on the restored synthetic copy returned the original lesson without rewriting evidence. That was not HTTP replay on restored data or the real off-host beta restore.

## Open scope and operational gates

- [ ] Reconcile the original blanket archived credit-hold language with verified work performed before archive. New lessons and purchases cannot be granted from archive; archive itself leaves credit/expiry/funding untouched. The allocator evaluates the earned lesson date, not the later invoice date, and can insert a first redemption for already-attested eligible pre-archive work. That is different from exact receipt replay or recognizing an already redeemed participant, and the frozen redemption guard does not check current archive state. Recommended distinction for review only: settle independently verified usage earned before archive without reactivation, while blocking new use during archive. No such policy choice is approved here. Review whether historically earned first settlement is explicitly preserved or all new redemptions are held until reactivation. The latter needs an authoritative database rule; an app-only filter would not enforce it.
