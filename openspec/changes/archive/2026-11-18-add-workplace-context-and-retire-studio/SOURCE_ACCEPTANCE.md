## Source crosswalk and independently observable behavior

| Surface | Canonical source and signed/browser evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Shared context, persistence and scope | TeacherNav, `lib/server/teacher-workplace-context.ts`, authenticated workplace context GET/SET and `ccp_workplace_context_get/set`; `parity-workplace-context` covers foreign ID, stale-write rejection, retry, replacement, deliberate All and an archived selection. `parity-workplace-personal-free` covers actual hard-delete to All. An existing tab can detect an unavailable old selection during refresh; a fresh tab cannot infer why a NULL was written. |
| Dashboard, students, history, reporting | Dashboard summary HTTP and `ccp_dashboard_summary` use explicit/current scope; `parity-workplace-dashboard` covers counters, distinct keyboard-activated Calendar link and Manage link, explicit venue and All. `parity-workplace-roster-scope` and `parity-workplace-report-scope` cover dated/archived enrollment, one learner across venues, historic origin and deduped All. |
| Calendar, Coaching, Learning | `parity-workplace-calendar-context` and `parity-workplace-coaching-context` verify mixed zones, recorded snapshot precedence, scoped defaults, changed context and no implicit draft writes. `parity-workplace-learning-context` verifies selection, reload, cross-navigation, Personal and All; lesson/material history and default selectors scope by attributed use, active learner discovery scopes Homework, while global material templates and durable assignment identities do not change. `parity-material-defaults` verifies explicit owned default targets and history. |
| Free Personal | `parity-workplace-personal-free` exercises multiple Personal groups, roster, confirmed attendance and approved materials against Free, with no new professional or payable authority. |
| Account/business settings, credentials, billing, portal and messages | `teacher_settings`, business fields, `ccp_teacher_settings_get/update`, and scoped credential handoff replace the retired Studio service. `parity-policy`, `parity-workplaces-extended`, `parity-portal-cancellation-policy`, the workplace cancellation tests and localized dashboard/error cases cover typed ownership, fallback, RTL, recipient-scoped policy, English/Hebrew/Russian copy and canonical error codes. Tenant-global login language, notifications and credentials do not derive from selected venue. Approved `compensation_agreements` and ledger operations remain separate authorities. Business text does not promise publishing private information in the guest portal. |
| Import, backup/export and finance boundary | Portable import cannot attest claimed cancellation; historic observed terms, identity and attribution are protected, and physical restore preserves them. See ADR-0021 and each exact hosted restore. Generic JSON, selected workspace or group creation does not confer finance, entitlement or recipient authority. |
